## Naming

Every name chosen in this repository — for files, scripts, directories,
functions, variables, classes, commits, branches, and documentation such
as the man page — must use precise professional vocabulary. Write the
word a 1972 IBM engineer would put in a specification. No slang, no
casual shorthand, no cute or clever names, no words borrowed from chat
culture, no filler ("say,", "and friends") or contractions in prose.
This applies to identifiers and to written prose alike, not naming alone.
